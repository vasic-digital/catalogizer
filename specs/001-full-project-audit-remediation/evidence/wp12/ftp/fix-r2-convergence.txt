  C2  INSTANCE-AT-A-TIME / MISSING CLASS ENUMERATION (cause 2). Deadlines were added to ONE data read (stream.Read) and nowhere else. The class is
      "every blocking I/O of the client". Members enumerated with a control needle: TCP connect, banner, AUTH TLS reply, TLS handshake, USER, PASS,

SURPRISES THAT CHANGED THE DESIGN (ground truth beats the model)
  S1  Rewrite instead of patch: the library offers no hook for any of C1; the plaintext of the control channel (needed to classify login phases)
      is not observable above TLS without owning the protocol layer. pkg/ftp now speaks the protocol (proto.go, mlsx.go); the library is no
      longer imported.
  S2  Real pure-ftpd answers "OPTS UTF8 ON" with "504 Unknown command" although it lists UTF8 in FEAT. My first design (fail the connect on a
      refusal, to avoid garbled names) broke EVERY real-server test; the design is now: connect, and refuse only a listing that really contains
      a garbled name (invalid UTF-8 or 0x7f). Found by the real-server leg, not by the fake.
  S3  Real pure-ftpd answers an early-closed TLS download with ONE reply "150 <statistics>" (not 226/426). My first design expected 226/426 after
      an early close and dropped the connection (30 s IOTimeout wait, then a re-dial per seek). The lock-step check (NOOP) is the guard now, the
      first reply may be anything below 400.
  S4  The fake itself listed "/" as a child of itself (a nameless entry the library silently dropped for months): the new parser surfaced it.
